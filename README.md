One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance — the mystery of the lone sock that comes out of the wash without its partner.

## The pitch

> The Sock Tribunal: a washing machine that puts every lone sock on trial, cross-examines the dryer under oath, and sentences the guilty lint to community service inside your remaining sock.

The pitch is exactly one sentence, 188 characters (limit: 220). The invention is absurd, but its fix is weirdly plausible: instead of shrugging at the missing sock, the machine investigates, interrogates the prime suspect (the dryer), and hands down a sentence — so laundry day finally delivers justice, not just damp disappointment.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (standard library only; verified with `python3 -S main.py`).
